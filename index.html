    FAQ

    Straight answers.

    What if the ads bring no sales?

    Then that month's fee is ₹0. There's no minimum fee. You only pay the one-time ₹5,000 setup.

    What counts as "sales"?

    The purchase value Meta Ads Manager reports, using Meta's standard count (purchases within 7 days of a click or 1 day of a view). It can differ from your store's orders, and isn't reduced for cancellations, returns, RTO or unpaid COD. You can check the same number in your own Ads Manager, and every invoice includes a screenshot.

    Why does my bank show an autopay limit of ₹1,33,339?

    That's a ceiling, not a charge. It covers the biggest possible month: the ₹1,00,000 fee cap plus the ₹12,999 call plan, plus GST. You're only ever debited your invoice amount.

    When am I billed?

    Your invoice is generated on the 3rd of each month for the previous month, with an Ads Manager screenshot as proof. Autopay debits it on the 7th.

    Do you work with non-Shopify websites or marketplaces?

    No. We work only on Shopify stores, and we don't run marketplace ads. No Shopify store yet? We can build one, starting at ₹10,000.

    Do you pay for my ads?

    No. You pay Meta directly from your own ad account. We never touch your ad spend.

    Who owns the ad account and data?

    You do. We only get partner access. Campaigns, pixel and data stay in your account, even after you leave.

    Can I cancel?

    Anytime, with 15 days' notice and no exit fee. We remove our partner access and everything we built stays with you.

    Do I need to create content?

    A little. We send scripts and shot lists, you film on your phone (or send what you already have), and we edit and test. Rachna can plan content you can shoot in under 30 minutes.

    Do you guarantee results?

    No one honestly can. What we can promise: experts on your account every day, ads checked every 3 hours, and no fee in a month with no sales.
